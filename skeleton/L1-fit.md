## L1 — Fit

Standing conditions. These do **not** decay — a company that runs open
models still runs them next quarter.

Required: `{{FIT_REQUIRED}}`
Plus at least one of:
{{FIT_ANY_OF}}

Fit is a gate, not a score. It answers "could they ever buy this?" The
window layer answers "are they buying now?"

A qualified row with no fit evidence is the single most common false
positive — it means the whole rationale rests on activity signals with
nothing establishing the company consumes this category at all.
