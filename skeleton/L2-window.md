---

**Tier placement is causal, not categorical.** The same signal type sits
in different tiers across builds. A leadership hire is Tier B where the
title is near-universal, and Tier A where that person's arrival directly
causes the purchase.

**Keyword signals read content, not volume.** Six job posts means the
company is hiring. Six job posts naming specific tooling means they are
building the thing that creates demand. Score the second.
